I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Konkuk University, working on geometric analysis and partial differential equations. I am particularly interested in geometric flows, convex geometry, free boundary problems, and nonlocal equations.

Before joining Konkuk, I was at KIAS (Korea Institute for Advanced Study) — first as a Research Fellow (2020–2023), then as a Project Research Fellow supported by the Sejong Science Fellowship (2023–2025), working with Kyeongsu Choi. I received my Ph.D. from Seoul National University in 2020 under the supervision of Ki-Ahm Lee, where my thesis focused on curvature flows with obstacles.
